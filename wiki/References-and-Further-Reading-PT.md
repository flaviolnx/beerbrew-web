# Referências e Leitura Complementar

Bibliografia técnica, artigos científicos e padrões da indústria utilizados na Wiki do **beerbrew-web**.

## Livros Fundamentais

* **Palmer, J. J. (2017).** *How to Brew: Everything You Need to Know to Brew Great Beer Every Time* (4ª ed.). Brewers Publications.
* **White, C., & Zainasheff, J. (2010).** *Yeast: The Practical Guide to Beer Fermentation*. Brewers Publications.
* **Palmer, J. J., & Kaminski, C. (2013).** *Water: A Comprehensive Guide for Brewers*. Brewers Publications.
* **Hieronymus, S. (2012).** *For the Love of Hops*. Brewers Publications.
* **Daniels, R. (1996).** *Designing Great Beers*. Brewers Publications.
* **Fix, G. (1999).** *Principles of Brewing Science* (2ª ed.). Brewers Publications.
* **Briggs, D. E., Boulton, C. A., Brookes, P. A., & Stevens, R. (2004).** *Brewing: Science and Practice*. Woodhead Publishing.
* **Kunze, W. (2014).** *Technology Brewing and Malting* (5ª ed.). VLB Berlin.
* **Noonan, G. J. (1996).** *New Brewing Lager Beer*. Brewers Publications.
